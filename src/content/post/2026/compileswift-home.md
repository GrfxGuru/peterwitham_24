If you went to CompileSwift.com and ended up here, you are not crazy. I decided long enough had gone by to face the fact that the podcast retired last year and I do not have the time to bring it back.

So rather than leave the visitors at a dead end I decided to redirect to this site.

Until now I had always hoped to find a way to make it work again, but the realization is that my life is too busy with a day job and everything else to find time to give it what it deserves should I bring it back.

Since so many folks know me by that user name it made the most sense to send them to my personal site (this one) and hope that I can continue to delight with content and maybe entertainment here.
