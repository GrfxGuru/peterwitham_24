We often forget about all those apps that come with our devices and operating systems. We also find a tool and stick with it, never going back to see if the latest update to an OS now has features that make our extra apps that we pay for no longer needed.

Some thoughts in this episode on going back to the free tools that come built-in and saving money and some times, the pain of managing 3rd party applications.
