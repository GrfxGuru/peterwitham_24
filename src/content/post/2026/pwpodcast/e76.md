 In this episode of the PW Podcast, I dive into the challenges of getting reliable software feedback. I argue that friends and family, while well-meaning, rarely give truly honest critiques.

Instead, I recommend finding testers from relevant communities with people who actually care about your app or game's niche.

It is also important to repeat feedback cycles, pushing updates back to the same testers and weighing results carefully against your original vision. The key takeaway: the right testers make all the difference.
