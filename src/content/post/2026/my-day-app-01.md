The app that I started building for myself and then decided others might have a need is really taking shape.

The idea is this. I wanted a simple app that allowed for multiple journals and journal types to track anything I need. In my case, the two most used are the daily journal and my development journal.

Naturally, this expanded to include my photography and then I realized that it made sense to include social post tracking since going to each network to see if I posted was time consuming and could be centralized.

I'll expand more on this app going forward, but for now here is a preview of the current state including multiple journals with custom colors and entries.
