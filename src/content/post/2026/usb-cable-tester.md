Why do you a tool that just tests USB cables?

[Treedix USB tester on Amazon](https://amzn.to/4zOkbGk)

Simple, not all cables are equal when it comes to performance and capabilities. USB-C for example, can charge a device and/or transfer data. And what about that fast charging? You cannot tell by looking at the cable.

And all those cables that come with devices like external drives are probably the cheapest the manufacturer could put in the box and get it to work. That does not mean you get the performance you expect.

I am going to be you've been there like the rest of us, you plug something in, it doesn't connect to your device or fails to charge.

That's where this [USB cable tester](https://amzn.to/4zOkbGk) comes in. It shocked me how many cables from USB-A, Mini-USB, USB-C and Thunderbolt I own are essentially useless.

The testing of a cable is easy, you plug both ends into the device, and it tells you the features it supports. That's it!
